Integration Boundaries

Composition tests may cross owners only after the crossed modules have their own owner proofs. Integration must use real owner APIs instead of a second fake runtime.

E2E Projections

  • SOCKS5, raw UDP, mesh-peer, resolver, scheduler, observer, runtime, and binary routes compose from module proofs.
  • Live WAN and throughput tests are evidence gates, not substitutes for owner correctness.
