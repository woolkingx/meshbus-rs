Owner Proofs

ProofRequired assertion
Kernel verification gateMalformed ids, unknown hooks/sinks, pipeline jumps, and invalid wiring fail at verify time.
Kernel opacityProtocol identifiers and L7 concepts are not leaked into kernel ownership.
Session dispatch safetyFrame metadata-only dispatch cannot parse protocol payloads and stays inside typed metadata/contracts.
