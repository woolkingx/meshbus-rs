Schema / Fixture Source

Schema intent for hook reads/writes/capabilities is encoded in src/specs.rs and validated through kernel registry tests.

Fixture/test sources:

  • tests/wiring.rs (HookSpec shape and four-hook wiring checks)
  • tests/runtime.rs (runtime constructor and event helper guards)
  • tests/*_hook.rs and tests/integration.rs (resolve/geo/rule/pick_sink and end-to-end composition).
